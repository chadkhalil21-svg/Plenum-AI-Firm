# Advisory Universe

Plenum AI Firm uses a **dynamic advisory universe**, not a frozen list or popularity ranking. The research worker selects authorities and exemplars relevant to the proposition and re-searches current sources.

## Mandatory / authoritative layer
Applicable law and regulators; formal standards bodies; platform/provider primary requirements; government technical guidance; professional and scientific societies where authoritative.

## Engineering, science & systems
NASA, NIST, W3C, relevant national laboratories and academies, IEEE/ACM and domain societies where applicable, and relevant current work from institutions such as MIT, Stanford, Harvard, Princeton, Carnegie Mellon, Caltech, UC Berkeley and other leading research universities chosen for the actual discipline.

## Business, operations & institutions
Relevant current research and practice from Harvard Business School, Stanford Graduate School of Business, MIT Sloan, Wharton, Kellogg, INSEAD and other institutions when materially relevant. Prestige alone is not evidence.

## Design, architecture, HCI & product
Platform guidance plus relevant work from leading practitioners/firms and research bodies. The comparative pool may include IDEO, Pentagram, frog, Designit, Nielsen Norman Group, Stanford d.school, Carnegie Mellon HCII, and other current specialists discovered for the problem. For physical architecture/industrial design, the worker must discover discipline-specific firms and practitioners rather than reusing a software list.

## Premium service / first-class hospitality
Use current service exemplars selected for the actual service problem: leading international first-class aviation and lounges, luxury hospitality, premium transportation, and other high-touch operations. Emirates is a standing reference, not the sole reference.

## Historical analytical council
Henry Ford, John D. Rockefeller, Steve Jobs and other historically relevant builders may be used as analytical lenses. Historical reputation is not current empirical evidence.

## Rule
"Top" is never established by assertion. The worker records why an institution, practitioner, firm, product, scientist, designer, architect, engineer, writer/editor, or service exemplar is relevant to the proposition, what evidence is being imported, its date, and its limitations. Multiple independent perspectives are preferred for consequential decisions.
