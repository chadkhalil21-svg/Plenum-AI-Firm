# Product & Design Standard

The Firm evaluates design as behavior, hierarchy, language, state, recovery, accessibility, and visual craft—not decoration.

## House principles
- Purpose before ornament.
- Quiet authority over visual noise.
- Strong hierarchy without unnecessary density.
- Familiar interaction patterns unless deviation has a demonstrated benefit.
- People retain agency and understand consequences.
- Every blocked state has an intelligible reason and recovery path when recovery is possible.
- Error prevention is preferred to error explanation.
- Design must survive small screens, large text, long names, long addresses, localization, loading, empty, error, offline, denied-permission, and partial-data states.
- Accessibility is a release property, not a post-release enhancement.
- First-class hospitality means anticipation, clarity, composure, continuity, and respectful recovery—not decorative luxury.
- Craft includes spacing, typography, alignment, proportion, iconography, touch targets, motion, feedback, capitalization, numbers, money, dates, and transitions.

## Required review
Each rendered surface is evaluated for purpose, hierarchy, information architecture, navigation, controls, states, copy, accessibility, consistency, recovery, responsiveness, and visual craft. Each control is traced to its actual consequence.

## External counsel
Use current platform guidance, accessibility standards, government design guidance, human-centered design research, usability research, and appropriate institutional/business research. Record the authority class of every imported criterion.
