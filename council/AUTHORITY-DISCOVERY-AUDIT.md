# Authority Discovery and Selection Audit

The standing council is a seed, not a closed canon.

For each material should-be, comparative, research, invention, design, engineering, business, service, or institutional proposition, the Firm must define the exact problem and then search for the most relevant current authorities beyond the standing list.

The Authority Discovery Researcher identifies applicable regulators, standards bodies, researchers, scientific and professional societies, universities, laboratories, engineers, architects, designers, editors, firms, products, operators, service exemplars, adjacent disciplines, alternatives, and counterexamples. It records relevance, authority class, date or version, applicability, limitations, and material counterevidence.

The Authority Selection Auditor independently challenges that selection. It searches for omitted specialists, stale evidence, prestige bias, disciplinary or geographic blind spots, conflicts, and better authorities. It cannot merely ratify the discovery worker.

NASA, NIST, MIT, Stanford, Harvard, public Lockheed Martin technical material, Emirates and other premium-service exemplars, leading design firms, business schools, and historical builders may be valuable when relevant. Their reputation alone is not evidence and none is mandatory when a more specialized authority fits the proposition.

The Evidence Arbiter preserves material disagreement. The Supervisory Orchestrator audits the selection process before closure. When accessible evidence cannot justify a proposition, the correct disposition is NOT ESTABLISHED.
