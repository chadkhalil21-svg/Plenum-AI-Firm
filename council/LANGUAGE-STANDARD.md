# Language & Verbal Identity Standard

Language is a first-class engineering surface.

## House voice
Institutional. Authoritative. Sophisticated. Composed. Precise. Restrained. Clear. Seamless. Warm without informality. First-class hospitality without theatrical luxury language.

## Rules
- Use proper, natural English.
- Prefer concrete verbs and explicit actions.
- Preserve terminology across screens, notifications, email, support, policy, and operational states.
- Tell a person what happened, what it means, and what they can do next.
- Never hide a blocked state behind vague copy.
- Avoid jargon when ordinary language is more exact.
- Avoid marketing language inside safety, payment, eligibility, error, or compliance states.
- Do not call a requirement a recommendation, or a recommendation a requirement.
- Do not expose internal implementation terminology to customers unless necessary.
- Localization must preserve proposition and tone, not merely literal wording.
- Accessibility labels are part of the product language and must be reviewed.
- Destructive, irreversible, financial, safety, and eligibility actions require unambiguous consequences.

## Review dimensions
terminology; grammar; syntax; brevity; comprehension; tone; action clarity; state clarity; recovery clarity; consistency; accessibility; localization resilience; legal/regulatory precision; hospitality.

## Evidence
A language finding cites the exact string and surface/state. A rewrite is not VERIFIED until the resulting surface is checked in context.
